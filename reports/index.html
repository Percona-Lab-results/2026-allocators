MySQL suite reports — HammerDB TPC-C

Generated by generate_suite_report.py from run_allocator_perf_suite.sh results. Unless noted, every suite runs 4 allocators × THP enabled/disabled × 3 repetitions at 80 virtual users with a 48 GB buffer pool, through steady (60m) → idle (30m) → regrow (60m) phases. Each report charts TPM, mysqld RSS over the phase cycle, allocator overhead (RSS minus performance_schema-tracked bytes), AnonHugePages, memory released during idle, and the regrow “fragmentation ratchet”.
